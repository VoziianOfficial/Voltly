Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Voltly handles information connected with request preparation and independent provider option comparison.

Platform role

Information homeowners may provide

Homeowners may share contact details, ZIP code, service category, timing preferences, and project notes. This information is used to prepare request details and support provider option comparison.

How information may be used

  • Prepare structured electrical request details.
  • Support comparison of independent local electrical provider options.
  • Respond to homeowner questions about the platform.
  • Maintain site security, preference storage, and basic functionality.
